Setup A (strict)
H1 EMA20 above or below EMA50 with EMA50 sloping the same way. Price retests an exact H1 swing level (within 2 pips). M15 closes a pin bar or engulfing candle. SL, TP and lot are set.

Setup B (looser)
H1 EMA20 above or below EMA50. Price retests a swing level, a round number (00 or 50) or the H1 EMA50 (within 5 pips). M15 also accepts a wick rejection, a reversal close or an EMA20 bounce. Same SL, TP and lot.
